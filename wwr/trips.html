Principal Trips Information

Lima Lunahuaná: A Hidden Gem of Adventure and Relaxation

Experience breathtaking views and adventure.

Location: Lunahuana, Lima, Peru

Date: June 15-20, 2025

Price: $1200 per person

Requirements: Hiking gear, moderate fitness level

Description: Nestled in the lush valleys of Peru, Lunahuaná is a paradise for adventure seekers and nature lovers. Renowned for its thrilling white-water rafting on the Cañete River, this charming destination also offers scenic vineyards, historic ruins, and a laid-back atmosphere perfect for unwinding. Whether you're tasting exquisite local wines, hiking through breathtaking landscapes, or exploring the rich history of the region, Lunahuaná promises an unforgettable experience.

Marañón River: The Grand Canyon of the Amazon

Experience breathtaking views and adventure.

Location: Marañon river, Peru

Date: June 15-20, 2025

Price: $1200 per person

Requirements: Hiking gear, moderate fitness level

Description: Flowing through the heart of Peru, the Marañón River is a hidden gem for adventurers seeking untouched landscapes and thrilling expeditions. Known as the "Grand Canyon of the Amazon," this river offers breathtaking canyons, wild rapids, and pristine jungle scenery. Whether you're rafting through its challenging waters, exploring remote indigenous communities, or camping under the stars, the Marañón promises an unforgettable journey into nature’s raw beauty.

Colca River Arequipa: Adventure in the Deepest Canyon

Experience breathtaking views and adventure.

Location:Colca River Arequipa Peru

Date: June 15-20, 2025

Price: $1200 per person

Requirements: Hiking gear, moderate fitness level

Description: Located in the heart of Arequipa, the Colca River flows through one of the world's deepest canyons, offering breathtaking scenery and thrilling white-water rafting. As you navigate its powerful rapids, you'll witness stunning Andean landscapes, towering cliffs, and even soaring condors. Whether you're an adrenaline junkie seeking an unforgettable rafting experience or a nature lover exploring the rich biodiversity of the region, the Colca River is a must-visit destination.
